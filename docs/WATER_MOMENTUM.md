# Targeted stone reaction coupling

The old coherent baseline supplied kinematic displaced volume only. `Session.advance` now differences the cumulative actual `Contact.netImpulse.y` after each fixed physics step, negates the force-on-stone impulse, and accumulates it until the next 120 Hz WaterTick. A magnitude-weighted contact centroid locates the source. Replay resets the measurement and pending accumulator; rendering drains commands once. No bounced/score gate is used. Final submerged contact continues to supply actual increments until physics ends at its existing -0.3 m cutoff, then water freely relaxes. Physics, launch sampling and immutable contact timing are unchanged.

For each water command, J = -delta(sum contact netImpulse.y), in N s. A Gaussian K_i = exp(-r_i²/(2 sigma²)), truncated at 3 sigma, is normalized over actual in-domain cell centers: Z = sum K_i A. A=(16/512)² m², sigma=1.25 cells=0.0390625 m (grid participation, NOT an enlarged physical stone). delta v_i = J K_i/(rho D Z) m/s; GPU green stores per-step height increment, so delta g_i = delta v_i /120 m. rho=1000 kg/m³; D=0.04 m is an explicitly empirical effective participating water depth, not measured lake depth (optical bed remains 0.65 m). sum rho D A delta v_i = J. The source enters velocity, never a scripted height ring. Existing propagation subsequently applies .995 damping, so after one step the integrated momentum is .995 J. Signed downward reaction makes a depression, propagation produces the crest.

The fixed normalized kernel resolves subcell input with roughly 20–50 contributing cells, avoiding vanishing point-sampled momentum. No grid or CPU solver enlargement or additional GPU passes. Its energy is finite and explicitly computable: E = J² sum(A K_i²)/(2 rho D Z²). This is not a measured fluid energy transfer or complete energy conservation claim. No arbitrary opacity/amplitude multiplier or momentum clipping; out-of-domain support is zero. Horizontal reaction is not yet coupled; this is a vertical shallow-layer surrogate, not Navier–Stokes. The kinematic volume source retains its existing cell-center sampling; only the new momentum is discretely conservative. Mouse remains explicitly kinematic with zero measured momentum, including a matched 8 cm comparison.

The velocity damping bounds the uniform height mode but does not enforce exact water-volume conservation: absent boundary flux, the asymptotic integrated height contribution is J_total * dt * .995 / (rho D (1-.995)).
