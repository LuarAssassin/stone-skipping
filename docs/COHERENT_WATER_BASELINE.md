# Coherent water baseline

## Default path and upstream mapping

Current correction: [WATER_MOMENTUM](WATER_MOMENTUM.md) documents the newly connected measured vertical contact reaction, discrete normalization, empirical depth, units and limitations. The following baseline equations still describe the retained displacement and propagation stages; the new source additionally updates green velocity before propagation. Visual acceptance remains unpassed.

`Session.advance` produces `WaterTick {old,next,momentum?}` at 120 Hz from the unchanged 4000 Hz rigid-body physics. `createFeedback.update` hands that queue and generation to `createWater.update`, which clears both GPU targets on a new generation, drains each command once, updates caustics and binds the resulting texture to the same default game mesh. Mouse validation uses the explicit bottom-left toggle, captures pointer input before the throw handler and creates exactly the same footprint command. Throw/replay buttons exit mouse mode. Pausing stops the visual clock; simulation end does not stop water relaxation. There is no second end-of-throw advance of the physics clock.

`CoherentWater` adapts Evan Wallace's `water.js` updateShader, normalShader and moveSphereShader; its caustic material adapts `renderer.js` refracted-ray mapping and derivative area ratio. `water.ts` retains Three Water planar reflection camera and shader, replacing its noise normal with the coherent field and adding screen-space transmission/absorption. Exact source links, retained reference source and Evan's full MIT text are recorded in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. This is not a port of Evan's entire raytraced pool renderer. The bed checker/grid and sky gradient/sun are self-authored procedural calibration assets, not upstream photographs.

## State, domain and equations

Two RGBA32F linear-filtered ping-pong textures, 512 × 256, cover x=[0,16] m and z=[-4,4] m. Channels are height h in metres, velocity-like increment v in metres per water tick, and normal x/z. Texel pitch is 0.03125 m on each axis. A 1024 × 512 half-float caustic target maps to the bed at y=-0.65 m. Mesh vertices densely cover the simulated domain; surrounding geometry is flat. Outside-domain sources are not recentered. Clamp-to-edge sampling gives reflective/non-absorbing boundaries; this is not an infinite lake.

For a source centered at (x,z,y), rotate the horizontal offset by -angle; divide its across component by aspect. Let a=radius, d=depth and rho²=(along/a)²+(across/a)². The adapted displacement kernel has t=d sqrt(max(0,1-rho²)) exp[-(1.5 max(0,abs(y)/d-1))^6] and D=max(0,min(0,y+t)-min(0,y-t)). The source pass is h←h+D_old-D_new. Old displacement is returned, new displacement removed, including mouse release and final sinking. This smooth finite-depth approximation is NOT an exact ellipsoid volume integral or a two-way hydrodynamic solver. Its vertical envelope decays as the source sinks; the remaining wave field then relaxes without an indefinitely frozen cavity. CPU `displaced` matches the GLSL equation.

For the real stone, rotate the physical semiaxes (.04,.0045,.03) m by its actual quaternion. The 2×2 horizontal projection covariance gives major/minor eigenvalues: radius=sqrt(lambda_max), aspect=sqrt(lambda_min/lambda_max), angle=atan2(2Cxz,Cxx-Czz)/2. Vertical depth=abs(thicknessAxis.y)+hypot(longAxis.y,shortAxis.y), matching the support used by physics `wetGeometry` for the tilted disk plus half-thickness. Thus orientation, horizontal extents and submersion are not taken from a fake sphere or enlarged splash mesh. Invalid/non-finite or non-positive shape commands are replaced by a dry footprint before uniforms are uploaded. Mouse uses radius .22 m and depth .075 m for a visible calibration source.

Each tick, after source displacement, compute hbar as the mean of four immediate neighbours, v←.995[v+2(hbar-h)], then h←h+v. Reconstruct normals from world-space finite differences (normal pass uses forward gradients; surface shading uses centered gradients). Time is exactly 1/120 s per water tick. Physical sampling is quantized to the next 1/4000 s boundary, in a repeating 34/33/33 pattern; it is independent of RAF grouping and playback rate.

## Stability basis and rendering

For a Fourier mode, L=2(hbar/h-1) lies in [-4,0]. The damped update has determinant .995 and trace 1+.995(1+L), spanning [-1.985,1.995], within the unit-disk stability bounds. The undamped equivalent has c dt/dx=1/sqrt(2), the 2D five-point CFL limit; damping moves the high-frequency boundary inward. There is no adaptive wave timestep, shallow-water advection, volume-conserving free-surface transport or physical wave-speed calibration. Short stone contacts and one-texel stone footprints remain under-resolved; GPU readback is essential rather than CPU metadata alone.

The caustic shader refracts a fixed light through state normals to the bed and uses oldArea/max(newArea,epsilon), clamped to [0,8]. Fresnel F0=.0204 blends reflected sky with screen-space transmitted bed; exponential RGB absorption uses path-depth difference. Screen-space transmission is approximate, may double-image the stone, and cannot recover offscreen geometry. Caustic focusing is a geometric approximation, not radiometry.

## Verification and limits

`tests/coherent.test.ts` covers projected dimensions, GPU uniform binding/invalid commands, full throw/sink/after-sink scheduling, normal/slow/bullet irregular-RAF command identity, generation reset and once-only queue draining. `tools/coherent-browser-check.js` runs inside the real default game renderer: GPU rotated footprint covariance and CPU/GLSL comparison, 480-tick replay state comparison at 4 seconds, reset-to-zero, repeated-target resource counts, linked programs and GL errors. Run with `alma iab eval "$(cat tools/coherent-browser-check.js)"` after opening port 5173. Retained results and matched-time scene captures are indexed in `deliverables/coherent-water-baseline/README.md`.

The 10 legacy effect integration tests remain explicitly skipped with retirement reasons; physical trajectory/contact/variation tests remain active. Legacy algorithm unit tests still run, but do not imply the old renderer is active. Historical visual seed/surface/sheet/breakup metadata is retained in snapshots but does not affect the baseline. All three visual controls are disabled.

This is one coherent single-valued heightfield. It cannot represent overturning water, airborne fluid, sheet/rim/ligament breakup, spray or droplets. Those objects and simulation are off by default. Four-neighbour grid anisotropy, reflective boundaries, source aliasing and approximate transmission remain visual/physical limitations; no airborne-fluid fidelity is claimed.
